What it does

Everything from ingest to a verified drive, grouped by the job in front of you.

Bring in your footage

🎞️

Start from your master

Drop in a ProRes, H.264, H.265, or DNxHR movie (.mov, .mp4, .mxf), or a DPX, TIFF, EXR, or PNG frame sequence. DCP Wizard handles the conversion to cinema JPEG 2000 for you.

🎬

Conform from your edit

Resolve reels straight from your timeline (EDL or Final Cut XML) and build a finished multi-reel DCP end to end, keeping the reel plan and manifest as artifacts.

Build the package

📦

SMPTE or Interop

Ship a modern SMPTE package or a legacy Interop one, whichever the cinema's server expects.

🖥️

2K, 4K, any frame rate

2K and 4K at standard rates, plus high frame rate up to 120 fps at 2K.

⚙️

CPU or GPU JPEG 2000

Encode through Grok on all available CPU cores, or enable its accelerator plugin from the app or with --gpu. The job log records whether the device started and how many frames it encoded.

🖼️

Hit the right frame

Scope, flat, or full. Crop, auto-crop, fill, rotate, flip, or letterbox so the source lands on the DCI raster the container asks for.

🎬

Multi-reel features

Split a long feature into reels automatically, at a length, at timecodes, or at chapter marks, with sample-accurate audio and clean subtitle boundaries at every reel.

🌍

Every language in one package

Build one package with a version per language: several playlists share the same picture and sound, each with its own subtitles or dub. A version file ships only what changed against an existing OV.

⚡

Pre-build check

Every reason a job cannot be packaged is raised before the encode starts, so a bad title or a bad subtitle fails in a second instead of after an hour of JPEG 2000. Advisory hints (channel count, first cue too early, unusual frame rate) show in a dialog you can build through.

🏷️

ISDCF content titles

Rebuild the title from what the package actually carries: content type, container, languages, channel ladder, territory, rating, resolution, studio, and date. Ratings and content version also land in the CPL.

🎞️

Idents and rating cards

Keep a library of head idents, tail idents, rating cards, and anti-piracy clips. They join as their own reels, conformed to the feature's raster, rate, and sound layout at build time.

Sound and subtitles

🔊

Immersive sound

Package Dolby Atmos alongside your PCM mix. Map any source channel to any DCP lane, fill a stereo source out to 6, 8, or 16 channels without upmixing, or label HI, VI, and sign-language video (ISDCF Doc 13).

📝

Subtitles from your edit

Package SRT, ASS/SSA, PAC, FCPXML, Matroska, or Interop PNG bitmaps as cinema timed text, or burn them into the picture as it encodes. Placement, RTL, wrap, font embedding, and appearance (size, colour, outline or shadow, fades) are yours to set.

💬

Closed captions

A separate ST 429-12 closed-caption track, the same input formats as open subtitles. Carried through reel splits, language versions, and version files. Every reel that needs a caption gets one, including a placeholder on a reel with nothing to show.

Preview before it ships

▶️

Source and package preview

Play the source or the finished DCP in the app. JPEG 2000 decodes in process through a CPU worker pool, or on the device when GPU mode is active. Grok handles DCP directories, CPLs, picture MXFs, and codestream folders. Other sources use libmpv.

📋

Play, inspect, queue

A finished build offers Play, Inspect (the Verify view, already running), and Reveal. Queue packages from Recent Projects or a finished build and they play one after another.

Color and HDR

🌈

HDR and Dolby Vision

create --hdr-dci turns an HDR10, HLG, PQ P3-D65, or Dolby Vision 8.1 master into a DCI HDR Addendum DCP: ST 2084 on the picture descriptor, the HDR-Metadata extension in the CPL, HDR1 in the content title. --hdr-source names the grade when the master's colour tags do not, --hdr-peak-nits says where the roll-off starts.

🎨

Correct cinema color

Rec.709, P3-D65, and P3-DCI convert to the XYZ colour space projectors expect, so what you graded is what plays.

Lock it down

🔐

Encryption and KDMs

Encrypt every essence: picture, sound, subtitles, closed captions, and Atmos, each with its own key. Cut a KDM per screen, batch a whole release, email KDMs from a cinema list, or re-wrap from a distribution KDM when a new venue is added.

Check and deliver

✅

Built-in quality check

DCP Doctor checks your package against SMPTE Bv2.1 and hands you an HTML report, including JPEG 2000 forensics and Leq(m) sound level against the content kind's limit, so problems surface before the package leaves your desk.

📤

Verified copy to drive

Copy to a CRU or USB cinema drive with a free-space check and a post-copy hash check, or upload the finished package to a theatre management system over sftp.

▶️

Export for review

Turn a finished DCP back into ProRes or H.264 to screen internally or send out for approval.

Automation

Run deliveries hands-off. The command-line version drives everything the app does, plus watch folders, a job queue, and an automation API for headless rooms.

# Raise every refusal and hint before committing to the encode dcpwizard create --title "My Film" --video movie.mov --output ./dcp --check # Build a DCP from every master that lands in a watched folder dcpwizard watch ./incoming --output ./packages --webhook-url https://pipeline/hook # Queue a job and let the daemon work through it (params are a full DcpConfig) dcpwizard batch add -T create-dcp -p '{"title":"My Film","standard":"Smpte","resolution":"TwoK","content_type":"Feature","frame_rate_num":24,"frame_rate_den":1,"max_bitrate_mbps":250,"encrypt":false,"stereo_3d":false,"container_width":2048,"container_height":858,"output_dir":"./dcp","j2k_dir":"./j2k","audio_input_order":"Canonical51","subtitle_language":"en","reel_length_minutes":0}' # Serve the automation API for headless or batch operation dcpwizard serve --bind 127.0.0.1:8080

Prefer to build it yourself? DCP Wizard is open source. Grab the code and compile.

RequiredRust toolchain
RequiredGrok (CPU JPEG 2000)
OptionalGrok accelerator plugin (GPU encode and preview decode)
RequiredOpenSSL
RequiredXerces-C
Optionalffmpeg (transcoding)
Optionalmpv (GUI preview)
# Command-line build git clone --recurse-submodules https://github.com/PostPerfection/dcpwizard.git cd dcpwizard/rust cargo build --release # Desktop app build cd gui pnpm install pnpm tauri build
